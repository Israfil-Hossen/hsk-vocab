Part IVChina Context

What you need to know about China itself: Islam's 1,300-year history there, what Chinese people actually believe today, the legal limits on foreigners, the Confucian bridges, and the Chinese Islamic vocabulary Hui Muslims use.

এই অংশে: চীনে ইসলামের প্রায় ১,৩০০ বছরের ইতিহাস, চীনারা আজ আসলে কী বিশ্বাস করে, বিদেশিদের জন্য আইনের সীমা, কনফুসীয় সেতু আর হুই মুসলিমদের ইসলামি শব্দভান্ডার। পড়া শেষে তুমি দেখাতে পারবে যে ইসলাম চীনের নিজের ইতিহাসেরই অংশ, এবং আইনের ভেতরে থেকে বন্ধুত্বের মাধ্যমে কথা বলতে পারবে।

History of Islam in China

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

Islam has been in China for about 1,375 years. Use this freely; Chinese people respect their own history and most have no idea Islam is part of it.

Arrival. The Old Book of Tang records the first Arab (大食 Dashi) embassy in 651 CE, the second year of Emperor Gaozong, sent by the caliph Uthman; more than thirty embassies followed by 798. Chinese Muslim tradition says the Companion Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas led the mission and founded the Huaisheng Mosque; historians find no evidence he reached China, so present 651 as “the date in Chinese dynastic history” and the Sa'd story as “beloved tradition”. That honesty plays well.

Zheng He 郑和 (1371-1433). Born into a Muslim family in Kunyang, Yunnan, descended from Sayyid Ajall Shams al-Din, the Bukharan governor of Yunnan under the Yuan; commanded the Ming treasure fleets (seven voyages, 1405-1433) to India, Arabia and East Africa. Every Chinese schoolchild knows his name; few know he was Muslim.

The Hui 回族. Descendants of Tang and Song Arab and Persian traders, plus Central Asians who came with the Mongols; under the Yuan, Muslims were the largest non-Chinese group and ranked just below the Mongols. 2020 census: 11.4 million Hui; about 11.8 million Uyghurs; ten Muslim ethnic groups in all.

Numbers today. Pew Research (30 August 2023, Measuring Religion in China): about 90% of adults in the ten traditionally Muslim groups identify as Muslim, giving about 17 million adult Muslims, roughly 2% of adults; official figures count about 20 million Muslims and about 35,000 mosques (63% of mosques are in Xinjiang). The Islamic Association of China (中国伊斯兰教协会, founded 1953) is the official body; its site is chinaislam.net.cn.

The Han Kitab scholars: Islam in Confucian terms

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

Your whole project was done once before, 350 years ago, by Chinese Muslims. The Han Kitab (汉克塔布, “Chinese books”) are Islamic works written in Chinese in the 17th and 18th centuries by scholars called Huiru 回儒, “Muslim Confucians”, trained in both traditions and centred on the mosque schools of Nanjing. They are the model for your dawah.

Scholar Dates Main works What he did বাংলা
Wang Daiyu 王岱舆 c. 1590-1658 Zhengjiao zhenquan 正教真诠 (1642), “The Real Commentary on the True Teaching” First major Chinese Islamic book; explained Islam in a Confucian register; defined 清真: 纯洁无染为“清”，诚一不二为“真” (“unstained purity is qing; sincere oneness without duality is zhen”) ওয়াং দাইয়ু; 正教真诠 = 'সত্য ধর্মের প্রকৃত ব্যাখ্যা'; চীনা ভাষায় প্রথম বড় ইসলামি গ্রন্থ; 清真-এর সংজ্ঞা দেন: নিষ্কলুষ পবিত্রতাই 'ছিং', আন্তরিক ও অদ্বিতীয় একত্বই 'চেন'
Ma Zhu 马注 17th century Qingzhen zhinan 清真指南, “Compass of Islam” Written for non-Muslim elites as well as Muslims মা চু; 清真指南 = 'ইসলামের দিশারি'; মুসলিমদের পাশাপাশি অমুসলিম শিক্ষিত সমাজের জন্যও লেখা
Liu Zhi 刘智 c. 1660-c. 1730 (Nanjing) Tianfang xingli 天方性理 (metaphysics), Tianfang dianli 天方典礼 (law and ritual), Tianfang zhisheng shilu 天方至圣实录 (life of the Prophet), Wugong shiyi 五功释义 (the five pillars) The most systematic attempt to harmonise Islam with Chinese thought; used Neo-Confucian terms (理, 气, 太极, 道) and took ritual (礼 li) as the common ground of Confucian order and Islamic worship লিউ চি; 天方性理 = ইসলামি দর্শন, 天方典礼 = ইসলামের বিধান ও আচার, 天方至圣实录 = মহানবীর জীবনী, 五功释义 = পাঁচ স্তম্ভের ব্যাখ্যা; চীনা দর্শনের শব্দ (理 নীতি, 气 প্রাণশক্তি, 太极 মহাচূড়ান্ত, 道 পথ) দিয়ে ইসলাম বোঝান, আর 礼 (আচার-শিষ্টাচার)-কে দুই ঐতিহ্যের মিলনক্ষেত্র ধরেন

Their vocabulary became the standard: 真主 (True Lord) and 真宰 (True Ruler) for Allah, avoiding 天 which could sound like an impersonal sky; 天方 (“Heavenly Square”) for the Kaaba and Arabia; 清真 for Islam and the mosque; 忠主孝亲 (“loyal to the Lord, filial to parents”) as the summary of duty; and Liu Zhi's pairing of the “five pillars of the way of Heaven” (天道五功: 念礼斋课朝; বাংলা: কালিমা, নামাজ, রোজা, জাকাত, হজ) with the “five relationships of the way of man” (人道五典: ruler-subject, father-son, husband-wife, elder-younger, friends).

The word 清真. Originally an ordinary word meaning pure and unadorned, used by Tang and Song poets. The Hongwu Emperor's 1368 百字赞 (“Hundred-character Eulogy” of the Prophet) for the Nanjing mosque contains 教名清真 ("the religion is named Qingzhen"; বাংলা: ধর্মের নাম 'ছিংচেন', অর্থাৎ পবিত্র ও সত্য), and after a Ming imperial plaque named Beijing's Dongsi mosque 清真寺 the term became general. Telling a Chinese friend that a Ming emperor wrote a eulogy of the Prophet is a strong moment.

Ma Jian 马坚 and the Chinese Quran. Ma Jian (1906-1978), Peking University professor and al-Azhar graduate, began his translation after returning to China in 1939, at first with Imam Ha Decheng. The full translation was published posthumously in 1981 by China Social Sciences Press (Beijing) and in a bilingual Arabic-Chinese edition in 1987 under the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Endowments, the only Chinese translation recognised by the King Fahd Complex in Madinah. It is the Chinese Quran used in every mosque in China.

Scholarship to read: Sachiko Murata, William Chittick and Tu Weiming, The Sage Learning of Liu Zhi (Harvard, 2009); James Frankel, Rectifying God's Name: Liu Zhi's Confucian Translation of Monotheism and Islamic Law (Hawaii, 2011); Zvi Ben-Dor Benite, The Dao of Muhammad (Harvard, 2005); Kristian Petersen, Interpreting Islam in China (Oxford, 2018).

সারকথা: প্রায় ৩৫০ বছর আগে চীনা মুসলিম পণ্ডিতরা (হুইরু) কনফুসীয় ভাষায় ইসলাম বুঝিয়েছিলেন; 真主, 清真-এর মতো শব্দ তাদেরই দেওয়া। তাদের পথই তোমার দাওয়াতের আদর্শ, আর বন্ধুকে মা চিয়ানের চীনা কুরআনের কথা বলতে পারবে।

What Chinese people actually believe

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

The typical Chinese listener is non-religious but not irreligious: no label, but likely believes in fengshui, may bow to Buddha before an exam, burns incense at Qingming, and says 老天爷 ("Heavens!"; বাংলা: 'হায় আসমানের মালিক!') without thinking. Pew calls this “Confucianism, Taoism and Chinese folk religion”. It is a far better starting point than militant atheism.

So never ask 你信教吗？ (Nǐ xìnjiào ma? "Do you believe in a religion?") first; ask about fate, heaven and ancestors.বাংলা 你信教吗？ মানে "তুমি কি কোনো ধর্ম মানো?"; প্রথমেই এটা জিজ্ঞেস কোরো না, আগে ভাগ্য, আসমান আর পূর্বপুরুষ নিয়ে কথা বলো।

Young people. The slang tells the story: 内卷 (involution, endless competition for less), 躺平 (lying flat; the 2021 forum post that went viral and was then censored), 佛系 (“Buddhist-style” detachment). In 2023 temple visits booked on Trip.com rose 310% year on year, half of them by people under 30; most were not Buddhists and had no specific wish; they came for relief from pressure. This is the meaning crisis the handbook's Lesson 7 speaks to.

How Chinese media frame Islam. A 2018 study (Luqiu and Yang, Asian Journal of Communication) found Chinese news coverage of Islam generally negative, with Muslims portrayed as prone to terrorism or as recipients of special treatment; a 2021 study of state media found global Islam framed as dangerous while domestic Muslim minorities are framed as “good minzu”. Contact changes this: a small study of 200 young Chinese found more contact with Muslims meant fewer negative stereotypes. Your friendship is the correction.

Common lay images of Islam (from experience, not surveys): Islam equals not eating pork; 清真 is just a food label; Islam is “the Arabs' religion” or “Xinjiang's religion”; Muslims and terrorism; Muslim women are oppressed. The handbook's Chinese-questions section answers each.

সারকথা: বেশিরভাগ চীনা কোনো ধর্মের নাম নেয় না, তবু ভাগ্য, ফেংশুই, আসমান ও পূর্বপুরুষে কিছুটা বিশ্বাস রাখে; তরুণরা চাপ আর অর্থহীনতায় ভুগছে। তাই সরাসরি ধর্মের প্রশ্ন না করে ভাগ্য ও জীবনের অর্থ নিয়ে কথা শুরু করো, আর বন্ধুত্ব দিয়ে ভুল ধারণা ভাঙো।

The legal rules for foreigners

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

This is a description of the published rules, not legal advice. Enforcement varies by city and by year.

Instrument Issuer In force What it does বাংলা
Provisions on the Administration of Religious Activities of Aliens within the PRC (境内外国人宗教活动管理规定) State Council Decree 144 31 January 1994 Parent rule: foreigners may not set up religious organisations, open religious schools, or “develop followers among Chinese citizens” চীনে বিদেশিদের ধর্মীয় কার্যক্রম ব্যবস্থাপনা বিধান (১৯৯৪): বিদেশিরা ধর্মীয় সংগঠন বা ধর্মীয় স্কুল খুলতে পারবে না, চীনা নাগরিকদের মধ্যে অনুসারী বানাতে পারবে না
Regulations on Religious Affairs (宗教事务条例), revised 2017 State Council 1 February 2018 General framework: religious activity at registered venues; religion not subject to foreign domination; online religious content needs a licence (2022 measures) ধর্মীয় বিষয়াবলি প্রবিধান (২০১৭ সংশোধিত, ২০১৮ থেকে কার্যকর): ধর্মীয় কাজ নিবন্ধিত স্থানে, ধর্ম বিদেশি নিয়ন্ত্রণের অধীন নয়, অনলাইনে ধর্মীয় কনটেন্টের জন্য লাইসেন্স লাগে
Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Provisions on the Administration of Religious Activities of Foreigners (外国人在中华人民共和国境内宗教活动管理规定实施细则) National Religious Affairs Administration Order 23 1 May 2025 (published 1 April 2025); 5 chapters, 38 articles Replaces the 2000 implementation rules; mostly consolidates rules already in force চীনে বিদেশিদের ধর্মীয় কার্যক্রম বিধানের বাস্তবায়ন বিধিমালা (১ মে ২০২৫ থেকে কার্যকর): ২০০০ সালের বিধিমালার জায়গা নিয়েছে, মূলত আগের নিয়মগুলোকে একত্র করেছে

What a foreigner may do (2025 rules as reported):

  • Attend services at legally registered mosques (and temples and churches). Article 4 says the state respects foreigners' freedom of religious belief.
  • Hold collective religious activities for foreigners at a registered site or approved temporary venue, with Chinese clergy presiding; the organisers must name three or more conveners, each with a residence permit valid for more than six months.
  • Give a sermon or lecture only on invitation through a national or provincial religious association, with the content submitted in advance (Article 20).
  • Bring in religious books and media for personal use (reports say up to ten copies without prior permission; article not verified).

What a foreigner may not do:

  • Preach or proselytise without authorisation (擅自传教).
  • Recruit Chinese citizens as followers (在中国公民中发展教徒).
  • Establish religious organisations, schools or training; produce, sell or distribute religious materials; accept religious donations.
  • Organise collective religious activities outside approved venues, or have foreign clergy preside without approval.
  • In serious cases the venue can be ordered to stop serving foreigners; fines and expulsion are reported in general terms.

What this means in practice for you: private friendship, answering questions people put to you, living your religion visibly and attending the mosque are the realistic path. Do not hand out booklets, do not post religious content for Chinese audiences on WeChat, Douyin or Bilibili, do not run a study circle for Chinese citizens, and do not discuss Xinjiang or politics. A Chinese friend who becomes interested can read Ma Jian's Quran, which is legally printed and sold inside China, and can go to the mosque and speak to the ahong himself.

天 Tian, Heaven; 天命 Mandate of Heaven Confucius treats Heaven as a moral authority; Mencius 7A1: know your heart, know your nature, know Heaven The Creator “Confucius respected Heaven; we say Heaven has a Lord.” The Han Kitab used 真主, not 天, to avoid an impersonal sky 天 = আসমান; 天命 = আসমানের আদেশ। কনফুসিয়াস আসমানকে সম্মান করতেন; আমরা বলি, আসমানেরও একজন রব আছেন
敬鬼神而远之 (Analects 6.22) Respect the spirits but keep them at a distance Rejecting superstition while affirming the unseen Islam also forbids fortune-telling and idol worship but affirms God and the unseen "আত্মা-দেবতাদের সম্মান করো, কিন্তু দূরে রাখো"; কুসংস্কার বর্জন, তবে অদৃশ্যে বিশ্বাস

良知 / 良心 (Mencius 7A15); the four sprouts (2A6) Humans are born with hearts of compassion, shame, respect and right-and-wrong Fitrah (30:30); the hadith that every child is born on the fitrah A 2025 comparative study (San Jose State, Comparative Philosophy) finds Wang Yangming's liangzhi and fitrah both posit an inborn moral faculty and both reject original depravity 良知 / 良心 = সহজাত নৈতিক জ্ঞান / বিবেক; ইসলামের ফিতরাতের সাথে মেলে
人之初，性本善 (Three Character Classic) People are born good No original sin “Islam agrees with Mencius, not with Xunzi” "জন্মের সময় মানুষের স্বভাব ভালো"; ইসলামেও আদি পাপ নেই
诚 cheng, sincerity (Zhongyong) “The Way of all Ways”, linking the Way of man and the Way of Heaven Ikhlas, sidq; Wang Daiyu's definition of 真 as 诚一不二 诚 = আন্তরিকতা; ইখলাস ও সিদক
孝 xiao, filial piety Core Confucian virtue Quran 17:23-24; 忠主孝亲 Islam honours parents and visits graves, but prays only to God 孝 = পিতামাতার প্রতি ভক্তি ও সেবা; 忠主孝亲 = আল্লাহর প্রতি অনুগত, পিতামাতার প্রতি কর্তব্যপরায়ণ
中庸 zhongyong, the middle way Confucian ideal of moderation Ummatan wasatan, “a middle nation” (2:143) 中庸 = মধ্যপন্থা; কুরআনের "মধ্যপন্থী উম্মাহ" (২:১৪৩)
