Chapter 1: Dawah in China: method, manners and the law

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

1.1 Who you are speaking to

Most Chinese people are not committed atheists. About 10% of Chinese adults formally identify with a religion, yet 47% believe in fengshui and 33% believe in the Buddha or a bodhisattva [1]. Many were taught atheism at school but still speak of fate (命 mìng), Heaven (天 tiān) and ancestors. Most have never thought seriously about God. Your first task is rarely to defeat atheism; it is to help someone think about the question for the first time.

Confucian values (honesty, respect for parents, modesty, family, self-discipline) are close to Islamic values, and Chinese Muslim scholars used them as a bridge 350 years ago [2]. Islam has been in China since 651 CE [3]. You are not bringing something foreign; you are pointing to something already part of China's story.

1.2 The law: what you may and may not do

Two sets of rules apply to you as a foreigner: the Regulations on Religious Affairs (in force since 1 February 2018) and the Detailed Rules on the religious activities of foreigners (National Religious Affairs Administration Order No. 23, in force since 1 May 2025) [4].

  • You may attend a registered mosque, join foreigner-only gatherings at approved venues, bring a few religious books for personal use, and live your faith openly.
  • You may not preach without authorisation, recruit Chinese citizens as followers, distribute or sell religious materials, set up any religious group or class, or post religious content online for Chinese audiences.

In practice: build friendships, answer questions people ask you, and when someone is seriously interested, introduce them to the ahong (imam) at a registered mosque. Never discuss Xinjiang or politics. Protect yourself and the people you talk to. Part IV, section 4 gives the details.

1.3 The method of the Quran

“Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction, and argue with them in a way that is best” (Quran 16:125). In practice:

  1. Friendship first. Share food (halal food is a natural topic), help people, be reliable. Chinese culture trusts people before arguments.
  2. Ask questions, do not lecture. “Do you think the universe has a beginning?” works better than a speech.
  3. Agree where you can. “Yes, science is important. Islam commands us to study it.” Then add the one point they are missing.
  4. One idea per conversation. Leave them with one thing to think about, not twenty.
  5. Respect Chinese civilisation. Never mock the Buddha, Confucius or China. Quote Confucius correctly and you earn respect immediately [2].
  6. Admit what you do not know. “Good question, let me find the answer” builds more trust than a weak answer.
  7. Use only evidence that survives checking. A Chinese student with a phone will check your claim in thirty seconds. Part II grades every piece of evidence; use the strong ones [5].
  8. Guidance is from God. “You cannot guide whom you love, but God guides whom He wills” (Quran 28:56). Your duty is to deliver the message clearly and kindly; results are not your responsibility.

Chapter 2: Why believe in God: six arguments

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

You do not need all six. Learn the first two deeply; they are enough for most conversations. The others are for people who push further. The Chinese course teaches these in Lessons 3 and 4.

2.1 The universe began, so it needs a cause

  • Everything that begins to exist has a cause. Nothing comes from nothing.
  • The universe began to exist. Modern cosmology places the beginning of space, time and matter about 13.8 billion years ago, and the Borde-Guth-Vilenkin theorem shows that any universe that has on average been expanding cannot be infinite in the past [1].
  • Therefore the universe has a cause. That cause is outside space, time and matter: timeless, non-physical, immensely powerful, and it chose to create, because a timeless cause producing a universe that begins in time implies a will. This is what we call God.
  • Quran 52:35-36: “Were they created by nothing, or were they the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? No, they have no certainty.” The three options (from nothing, self-created, created by a Creator) are the whole debate in two verses [2].

中文: 宇宙有开始。有开始的东西一定有原因。所以宇宙有一个超越时间和空间的创造者。(Yǔzhòu yǒu kāishǐ. Yǒu kāishǐ de dōngxi yídìng yǒu yuányīn. Suǒyǐ yǔzhòu yǒu yí ge chāoyuè shíjiān hé kōngjiān de chuàngzàozhě.) “The universe had a beginning. Whatever begins must have a cause. So the universe has a Creator beyond time and space.”বাংলা মহাবিশ্বের একটা শুরু আছে। যার শুরু আছে, তার অবশ্যই একটা কারণ আছে। তাই মহাবিশ্বের একজন স্রষ্টা আছেন, যিনি সময় ও স্থানের ঊর্ধ্বে।

2.2 The universe is fine-tuned for life

  • The constants of physics sit inside very narrow ranges. The cosmological constant is often described as tuned to about one part in 10^120; a small change in the strength of the strong nuclear force would prevent the formation of carbon or of stable atoms [3].
  • There are three explanations: chance, necessity or design. Chance of this size is not a serious answer. Physics does not say the constants had to have these values. Design is the natural reading.
  • The multiverse is the usual escape, but it is unobserved, and a universe-generator would itself need fine-tuning, which moves the problem up one level [4].
  • Quran 67:3: “You do not see in the creation of the Most Merciful any inconsistency. So return your vision to the sky: do you see any flaw?”

中文: 物理常数非常精确，稍微改变一点，生命就不可能存在。这不像巧合，更像设计。(Wùlǐ chángshù fēicháng jīngquè, shāowēi gǎibiàn yìdiǎn, shēngmìng jiù bù kěnéng cúnzài. Zhè bú xiàng qiǎohé, gèng xiàng shèjì.) “The physical constants are extremely precise. Change them slightly and life is impossible. This does not look like coincidence; it looks like design.”বাংলা পদার্থবিজ্ঞানের ধ্রুবকগুলো অত্যন্ত নিখুঁত; সামান্য বদলালেই জীবন টিকে থাকা অসম্ভব। এটা কাকতালীয় মনে হয় না, বরং পরিকল্পনা মনে হয়।

2.3 Information and order in life

  • DNA is a code: four chemical letters, about three billion of them in the human genome, read by molecular machines with error correction [5]. Every code whose origin we know comes from a mind.
  • Evolution, even if fully true, explains how life changes, not how the first self-copying life or the laws that allow it began. The origin of life remains an open problem in science [6].
  • The Quran commands reflection on creation as a path to faith (3:190-191) and describes the stages of the embryo (23:12-14).

中文: DNA 是一种密码。我们知道来源的所有密码都来自智慧。(DNA shì yì zhǒng mìmǎ. Wǒmen zhīdào láiyuán de suǒyǒu mìmǎ dōu láizì zhìhuì.) “DNA is a code. Every code whose source we know comes from intelligence.”বাংলা DNA এক ধরনের সংকেত (কোড)। যেসব সংকেতের উৎস আমরা জানি, সেগুলো সবই কোনো বুদ্ধিমান সত্তা থেকে এসেছে।

2.4 Objective morality needs a Lawgiver

  • Almost everyone agrees that torturing a child for fun is really wrong, not just unfashionable. That is an objective moral fact.
  • Without God, morality is only chemistry, culture or a survival strategy. Then “wrong” means “most people dislike it”, and a society that decides otherwise is not actually wrong.
  • Objective moral duties make sense if there is a Lawgiver above all humans. Atheists can be good people; the point is that atheism cannot explain why goodness is binding [7].
  • Chinese bridge: Mencius taught that humans are born with a good conscience (良心 liángxīn) and innate moral knowledge (良知 liángzhī) [8]. Islam calls this the fitrah, the nature God placed in every person (Quran 30:30).

中文: 如果没有真主，善恶只是个人意见。但我们都知道，有些事是真正的错。(Rúguǒ méiyǒu Zhēnzhǔ, shàn'è zhǐ shì gèrén yìjiàn. Dàn wǒmen dōu zhīdào, yǒu xiē shì shì zhēnzhèng de cuò.) “If there is no God, good and evil are only opinion. But we all know some things are truly wrong.”বাংলা যদি আল্লাহ না থাকেন, তাহলে ভালো-মন্দ শুধু ব্যক্তিগত মত। কিন্তু আমরা সবাই জানি, কিছু কাজ সত্যিই অন্যায়।

2.5 Consciousness and reason

  • Atoms do not think, feel or understand. No physical description captures what it is like to experience something; philosophers call this the hard problem of consciousness [9].
  • If the mind were only atoms moved by physical laws, your beliefs, including atheism, would be the result of chemistry, not reasoning. Why trust them? A self that understands truth points beyond matter. Islam teaches that the soul is from God's command (Quran 17:85).

中文: 原子不会思考。为什么你会？(Yuánzǐ bú huì sīkǎo. Wèishénme nǐ huì?) “Atoms cannot think. Why can you?”বাংলা পরমাণু চিন্তা করতে পারে না। তাহলে তুমি পারো কেন?

2.6 The fitrah: belief is natural, atheism is learned

  • Children across cultures naturally see purpose and a maker behind the world; psychologists have described them as “intuitive theists” [10]. Almost every civilisation in history believed in a higher power. Atheism must be taught.
  • The Prophet said: “Every child is born upon the fitrah” [11]. In danger, even people who deny God call on Him (Quran 10:22, 29:65).
  • Chinese people say 谢天谢地 (“thank heaven and earth”) and 老天爷 (“Old Heavenly Lord”). This is the fitrah speaking through the language.

中文: 你们说“老天爷”、“谢天谢地”。你们的心其实已经知道有一个更高的主宰。(Nǐmen shuō “Lǎotiānyé”, “xiè tiān xiè dì”. Nǐmen de xīn qíshí yǐjīng zhīdào yǒu yí ge gèng gāo de zhǔzǎi.) “You say ‘Old Heavenly Lord’ and ‘thank heaven and earth’. Your heart already knows there is a higher Master.”বাংলা তোমরা বলো “লাওথিয়েনইয়ে” (বুড়ো আসমানি প্রভু) আর “আসমান-জমিনকে ধন্যবাদ”। তোমাদের মন আসলে আগে থেকেই জানে যে একজন উচ্চতর প্রভু আছেন।

Q1. “If everything needs a cause, who created God?” 谁创造了真主？

বাংলা: “আল্লাহকে কে সৃষ্টি করেছে?”

The argument never said “everything needs a cause”. It said “everything that begins needs a cause”. God does not begin; He is eternal and outside time. Asking “who created the uncreated?” is like asking “what is north of the North Pole?”. An endless chain of creators is impossible too: if every creator needed a creator, nothing would ever start, like a line of soldiers each waiting for the one behind to fire first. The chain must end in one uncreated Creator [1]. Quran 112:3: “He neither begets nor is born.”

中文: 我们说的是“有开始的东西需要原因”。真主没有开始，他是永恒的，在时间之外。如果每个创造者都需要创造者，那什么都不会存在。(Wǒmen shuō de shì “yǒu kāishǐ de dōngxi xūyào yuányīn”. Zhēnzhǔ méiyǒu kāishǐ, tā shì yǒnghéng de, zài shíjiān zhī wài. Rúguǒ měi ge chuàngzàozhě dōu xūyào chuàngzàozhě, nà shénme dōu bú huì cúnzài.) “We say “whatever has a beginning needs a cause”. God has no beginning; He is eternal, outside time. If every creator needed a creator, nothing would exist.”বাংলা আমরা বলি, “যার শুরু আছে, তার কারণ দরকার।” আল্লাহর কোনো শুরু নেই; তিনি চিরন্তন, সময়ের বাইরে। প্রত্যেক স্রষ্টার যদি আরেকজন স্রষ্টা লাগত, তাহলে কিছুই অস্তিত্বে আসত না।

Q2. “Science explains everything. Religion is unscientific.” 科学能解释一切

বাংলা: “বিজ্ঞান সবকিছু ব্যাখ্যা করতে পারে।”

Science explains how things work inside the universe. It cannot explain why the universe exists, why its laws exist, why mathematics describes it, or what is right and wrong. “Only science gives knowledge” is itself not a scientific statement; no experiment proves it. Islam has no conflict with science: the first word revealed was “Read” (96:1). Ibn al-Haytham's Book of Optics (c. 1011-1021) used systematic experiment centuries before Europe; the word “algebra” comes from al-Khwarizmi's book al-Jabr (c. 820); Ibn Sina's Canon of Medicine was taught in European universities for 500 years [2]. Many scientists believe in God, including Francis Collins, who led the Human Genome Project [3].

中文: 科学解释宇宙怎么运行，但不能解释宇宙为什么存在。伊斯兰鼓励科学，《古兰经》的第一个字是“诵读”。“代数”这个词就来自穆斯林科学家。(Kēxué jiěshì yǔzhòu zěnme yùnxíng, dàn bù néng jiěshì yǔzhòu wèishénme cúnzài. Yīsīlán gǔlì kēxué, 《Gǔlánjīng》 de dì-yī ge zì shì “sòngdú”. “Dàishù” zhège cí jiù láizì Mùsīlín kēxuéjiā.) “Science explains how the universe works, but not why the universe exists. Islam encourages science; the first word of the Quran is “Read”. The word “algebra” comes from a Muslim scientist.”বাংলা বিজ্ঞান ব্যাখ্যা করে মহাবিশ্ব কীভাবে চলে, কিন্তু মহাবিশ্ব কেন আছে তা ব্যাখ্যা করতে পারে না। ইসলাম বিজ্ঞানকে উৎসাহ দেয়; কুরআনের প্রথম শব্দ “পড়ো”। “অ্যালজেবরা” (বীজগণিত) শব্দটি এসেছে একজন মুসলিম বিজ্ঞানীর কাছ থেকে।

Q3. “Evolution proves there is no God.” 进化论证明没有神

বাংলা: “বিবর্তনবাদ প্রমাণ করে যে কোনো স্রষ্টা নেই।”

Evolution, even if accepted completely, describes a process. A process needs laws, a starting point and a universe that allows it. It does not explain where the first life, the laws or the universe came from. Natural selection is not a creator; it only removes what fails. Muslim scholars hold that nothing in scripture denies common ancestry for other life; Adam's special creation is a claim about one lineage that science, which studies regularities, cannot rule out [4]. The real question is whether the whole system (universe, laws and life) is an accident or a creation.

中文: 就算进化论完全正确，它也只是一个过程。过程需要规律和起点。进化论不能解释第一个生命从哪里来，也不能解释宇宙从哪里来。(Jiùsuàn jìnhuàlùn wánquán zhèngquè, tā yě zhǐ shì yí ge guòchéng. Guòchéng xūyào guīlǜ hé qǐdiǎn. Jìnhuàlùn bù néng jiěshì dì-yī ge shēngmìng cóng nǎlǐ lái, yě bù néng jiěshì yǔzhòu cóng nǎlǐ lái.) “Even if evolution is completely correct, it is only a process. A process needs laws and a starting point. Evolution cannot explain where the first life came from, or where the universe came from.”বাংলা বিবর্তনবাদ পুরোপুরি সঠিক হলেও এটা কেবল একটা প্রক্রিয়া। প্রক্রিয়ার জন্য নিয়ম আর একটা শুরুর বিন্দু লাগে। প্রথম প্রাণ কোথা থেকে এল, মহাবিশ্ব কোথা থেকে এল, বিবর্তনবাদ তা ব্যাখ্যা করতে পারে না।

Q4. “The Big Bang created everything. No God needed.” 宇宙大爆炸创造了一切

বাংলা: “মহাবিস্ফোরণ (বিগ ব্যাং) সবকিছু সৃষ্টি করেছে।”

The Big Bang is evidence for a beginning, which supports a Creator. Before it was accepted in the 1960s, many atheists preferred an eternal universe. An explosion is an event, not a cause: what caused it? And the “nothing” of popular physics books, the quantum vacuum, is not nothing; it is a field with laws and energy that itself needs explaining [5]. Quran 21:30: “Do the disbelievers not see that the heavens and the earth were a joined entity, and We separated them?”

中文: 大爆炸是一个事件，不是一个原因。是什么引起了大爆炸？大爆炸说明宇宙有开始，这正是有创造者的证据。(Dàbàozhà shì yí ge shìjiàn, bú shì yí ge yuányīn. Shì shénme yǐnqǐ le dàbàozhà? Dàbàozhà shuōmíng yǔzhòu yǒu kāishǐ, zhè zhèng shì yǒu chuàngzàozhě de zhèngjù.) “The Big Bang is an event, not a cause. What caused the Big Bang? The Big Bang shows the universe has a beginning, and that is exactly evidence for a Creator.”বাংলা মহাবিস্ফোরণ (বিগ ব্যাং) একটা ঘটনা, কারণ নয়। মহাবিস্ফোরণ ঘটাল কে? মহাবিস্ফোরণ দেখায় যে মহাবিশ্বের শুরু আছে, আর এটাই স্রষ্টা থাকার প্রমাণ।

Q5. “I cannot see God, so there is no evidence.” 我看不见真主

বাংলা: “আমি আল্লাহকে দেখতে পাই না।”

You cannot see gravity, electrons, the past, your own mind or your parents' love. You know them from their effects. We know the Creator from His effects: the universe, its order, its laws, our conscience. A watch proves a watchmaker even if you never meet him. The Quran praises those who “believe in the unseen” (2:3) on the basis of signs, not blind faith. And if God were a visible object inside the universe, He would be part of creation, not the Creator.

中文: 你也看不见引力、看不见电子、看不见你自己的思想，但你相信它们存在，因为你看到了它们的作用。我们通过创造认识创造者。(Nǐ yě kàn bú jiàn yǐnlì, kàn bú jiàn diànzǐ, kàn bú jiàn nǐ zìjǐ de sīxiǎng, dàn nǐ xiāngxìn tāmen cúnzài, yīnwèi nǐ kàndào le tāmen de zuòyòng. Wǒmen tōngguò chuàngzào rènshi chuàngzàozhě.) “You cannot see gravity, electrons or your own thoughts either, but you believe they exist because you see their effects. We know the Creator through creation.”বাংলা তুমিও মাধ্যাকর্ষণ, ইলেকট্রন বা নিজের চিন্তা দেখতে পাও না, তবু বিশ্বাস করো এগুলো আছে, কারণ তুমি এদের প্রভাব দেখো। আমরা সৃষ্টির মাধ্যমে স্রষ্টাকে চিনি।

Q6. “If God is good and powerful, why is there suffering?” 为什么有痛苦？

বাংলা: “দুঃখকষ্ট কেন আছে?”

This is the most emotional question, so answer gently [6]:

  1. Suffering disproves God only if God could have no good reason to allow it. A limited human cannot see all reasons, just as a child cannot understand why a doctor gives an injection.
  2. This life is a test, not paradise (Quran 67:2). Free will means people can choose evil, and most suffering comes from human choices: war, greed, oppression. Remove free will and you remove love, courage and every virtue.
  3. Suffering produces patience, compassion and humility. The Prophet said no hardship, illness or sorrow befalls a believer without God removing some of his sins by it [7].
  4. Islam promises perfect justice in the next life: every wrong repaid, every pain compensated (Quran 99:7-8). Atheism has no answer at all: the victim simply dies and the oppressor wins.

中文: 这个世界是考验，不是天堂。大部分痛苦来自人的选择。如果没有真主，受害者永远得不到公正。伊斯兰说，每一个不公都会在后世得到公正。(Zhège shìjiè shì kǎoyàn, bú shì tiāntáng. Dàbùfen tòngkǔ láizì rén de xuǎnzé. Rúguǒ méiyǒu Zhēnzhǔ, shòuhàizhě yǒngyuǎn dé bú dào gōngzhèng. Yīsīlán shuō, měi yí ge bùgōng dōu huì zài hòushì dédào gōngzhèng.) “This world is a test, not paradise. Most suffering comes from human choices. If there is no God, victims never get justice. Islam says every injustice will receive justice in the next life.”বাংলা এই দুনিয়া পরীক্ষা, জান্নাত নয়। বেশিরভাগ কষ্ট আসে মানুষের নিজের সিদ্ধান্ত থেকে। আল্লাহ না থাকলে নির্যাতিতরা কখনো ন্যায়বিচার পাবে না। ইসলাম বলে, প্রতিটি অবিচারের ন্যায়বিচার আখিরাতে হবে।

Q7. “Religion causes war and hatred.” 宗教引起战争

বাংলা: “ধর্ম যুদ্ধ বাধায়।”

The Encyclopedia of Wars lists 1,763 wars in history and classes 123 of them, about 7%, as religious [8]. The deadliest century was the 20th, dominated by secular ideologies [9]. People fight over land, power and money and sometimes use religion as a flag. Islam forbids killing innocents (5:32: killing one innocent is like killing all mankind), forbids forced conversion (2:256), and commands justice even toward enemies (5:8). Judge Islam by its texts, not by people who break them.

In China, make the general point (ideologies have killed more than religions) and move on. Do not criticise the Chinese state.

中文: 历史上只有大约百分之七的战争是因为宗教。人为了土地、权力和钱而战争。《古兰经》说：杀一个无辜的人，就像杀了全人类。(Lìshǐ shang zhǐ yǒu dàyuē bǎi fēn zhī qī de zhànzhēng shì yīnwèi zōngjiào. Rén wèi le tǔdì, quánlì hé qián ér zhànzhēng. 《Gǔlánjīng》 shuō: shā yí ge wúgū de rén, jiù xiàng shā le quán rénlèi.) “In history only about 7% of wars were because of religion. People fight for land, power and money. The Quran says: killing one innocent person is like killing all humanity.”বাংলা ইতিহাসে মাত্র প্রায় সাত শতাংশ যুদ্ধ হয়েছে ধর্মের কারণে। মানুষ যুদ্ধ করে জমি, ক্ষমতা আর টাকার জন্য। কুরআন বলে: একজন নিরপরাধ মানুষকে হত্যা করা যেন গোটা মানবজাতিকে হত্যা করা।

Q8. “I can be a good person without religion.” 没有宗教我也可以是好人

বাংলা: “ধর্ম ছাড়াও আমি ভালো মানুষ হতে পারি।”

Agree warmly: yes, you can, because God gave every human a conscience. But two questions follow. First, why be good when nobody is watching and being bad would benefit you? Atheism has no answer except personal taste. Second, who defines “good”? Societies have disagreed (slavery was once called good); without a standard above humans, “good” changes with fashion [10]. And being good is not the whole purpose of life. The purpose is to know and thank the One who gave you life.

中文: 是的，你可以是好人，因为真主给了每个人良心。但是，谁来定义“好”？还有，人生的目的不只是做好人，还要认识和感谢给你生命的那一位。(Shì de, nǐ kěyǐ shì hǎorén, yīnwèi Zhēnzhǔ gěi le měi ge rén liángxīn. Dànshì, shéi lái dìngyì “hǎo”? Háiyǒu, rénshēng de mùdì bù zhǐ shì zuò hǎorén, hái yào rènshi hé gǎnxiè gěi nǐ shēngmìng de nà yí wèi.) “Yes, you can be a good person, because God gave everyone a conscience. But who defines “good”? And the purpose of life is not only to be a good person, but also to know and thank the One who gave you life.”বাংলা হ্যাঁ, তুমি ভালো মানুষ হতে পারো, কারণ আল্লাহ প্রত্যেক মানুষকে বিবেক দিয়েছেন। কিন্তু “ভালো” কী, তা ঠিক করবে কে? তা ছাড়া জীবনের উদ্দেশ্য শুধু ভালো মানুষ হওয়া নয়; যিনি তোমাকে জীবন দিয়েছেন, তাঁকে চেনা আর তাঁর শুকরিয়া আদায় করাও।

Q9. “There are thousands of religions. They cannot all be right.” 那么多宗教，哪个是对的？

বাংলা: “এত ধর্ম, এর মধ্যে কোনটা সঠিক?”

Many answers do not mean no answer is right; a maths problem has many wrong answers and one right one. Most religions agree on the core: a higher power, a purpose, a judgement. Islam's position is that God sent the same message to every people (“there was no nation but a warner passed among them”, 35:24; also 16:36), people changed it over time, and the Quran is the final, preserved version. So the question is not “which of 4,000 religions?” but “which message about the One God is still unchanged?”

中文: 有很多答案，不等于没有正确答案。数学题有很多错的答案，但只有一个对的。伊斯兰说，真主给每个民族都派过使者，传的是同一个信息。(Yǒu hěn duō dá'àn, bù děngyú méiyǒu zhèngquè dá'àn. Shùxué tí yǒu hěn duō cuò de dá'àn, dàn zhǐ yǒu yí ge duì de. Yīsīlán shuō, Zhēnzhǔ gěi měi ge mínzú dōu pài guò shǐzhě, chuán de shì tóng yí ge xìnxī.) “Many answers do not mean there is no right answer. A maths problem has many wrong answers but only one right one. Islam says God sent messengers to every people, carrying the same message.”বাংলা অনেক উত্তর থাকা মানে সঠিক উত্তর নেই, তা নয়। অঙ্কের একটা প্রশ্নের অনেক ভুল উত্তর হয়, কিন্তু সঠিক উত্তর একটাই। ইসলাম বলে, আল্লাহ প্রত্যেক জাতির কাছে রাসূল পাঠিয়েছেন, সবাই একই বার্তা এনেছেন।

Q10. “You only believe because you were born into it.” 你信教只是因为你生在那里

বাংলা: “তুমি ধর্ম মানো শুধু এজন্য যে তুমি সেখানে জন্মেছ।”

This is the genetic fallacy: where a belief came from says nothing about whether it is true. A Chinese atheist believes what Chinese schools taught; that does not make atheism true or false either. People also enter Islam as adults from every background, including former atheists and scientists (Part II, section 8). Turn it into a question: “Have you examined what you were taught?”

中文: 你不信教，也是因为你生在中国，学校这样教你。一个信念从哪里来，和它对不对是两件事。重要的是：你有没有自己认真想过？(Nǐ bú xìnjiào, yě shì yīnwèi nǐ shēng zài Zhōngguó, xuéxiào zhèyàng jiāo nǐ. Yí ge xìnniàn cóng nǎlǐ lái, hé tā duì bú duì shì liǎng jiàn shì. Zhòngyào de shì: nǐ yǒu méiyǒu zìjǐ rènzhēn xiǎng guo?) “You do not believe in religion also because you were born in China and school taught you so. Where a belief comes from and whether it is right are two different things. What matters is: have you thought about it seriously yourself?”বাংলা তুমি যে ধর্ম মানো না, সেটাও তো এজন্য যে তুমি চীনে জন্মেছ আর স্কুল তোমাকে এভাবে শিখিয়েছে। একটা বিশ্বাস কোথা থেকে এল আর সেটা সঠিক কি না, এ দুটো আলাদা ব্যাপার। আসল কথা হলো: তুমি নিজে কি কখনো গুরুত্ব দিয়ে ভেবেছ?

Q11. “Religion is the opium of the people, for the weak and uneducated.” 宗教是人民的鸦片

বাংলা: “ধর্ম হলো জনগণের আফিম।”

Marx's line (1844) is a sociological opinion, not an argument about whether God exists; the same sentence calls religion “the sigh of the oppressed creature” [11]. The Quran commands knowledge: “Are those who know equal to those who do not know?” (39:9). The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, founded by a Muslim woman, Fatima al-Fihri, in 859, is recognised as the oldest existing, continually operating degree-granting institution in the world [12]. Comfort is not the test of truth, and the charge reverses: believing there will be no judgement is very comforting for someone who wants to do as he likes. Religion is not a crutch but a map: where you came from and where you are going.

中文: 《古兰经》说：“有知识的人和没有知识的人能相等吗？”世界上至今还在办学的最古老的大学，是一位穆斯林女性创办的。宗教不是拐杖，是地图：告诉你从哪里来，到哪里去。(《Gǔlánjīng》 shuō: “Yǒu zhīshi de rén hé méiyǒu zhīshi de rén néng xiāngděng ma?” Shìjiè shang zhìjīn hái zài bànxué de zuì gǔlǎo de dàxué, shì yí wèi Mùsīlín nǚxìng chuàngbàn de. Zōngjiào bú shì guǎizhàng, shì dìtú: gàosu nǐ cóng nǎlǐ lái, dào nǎlǐ qù.) “The Quran says: “Are those who have knowledge equal to those who do not?” The oldest university in the world still teaching today was founded by a Muslim woman. Religion is not a crutch but a map: it tells you where you came from and where you are going.”বাংলা কুরআন বলে: “যারা জানে আর যারা জানে না, তারা কি সমান হতে পারে?” পৃথিবীতে এখনো চালু থাকা সবচেয়ে পুরোনো বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়টি একজন মুসলিম নারী প্রতিষ্ঠা করেছিলেন। ধর্ম ক্রাচ নয়, মানচিত্র: তুমি কোথা থেকে এসেছ আর কোথায় যাচ্ছ, তা বলে দেয়।

Q12. “Why does God need our worship? Is He insecure?” 真主为什么需要我们崇拜他？

বাংলা: “আল্লাহর কেন আমাদের ইবাদতের দরকার?”

God needs nothing: “You are the ones in need of God; God is Free of need” (35:15; also 51:56-57). Worship benefits us, not Him. The sun does not need you to face it, but you need it to feel warm. Worship is gratitude to the One who gave you eyes, parents and every breath; it reminds you of your purpose and keeps you humble and honest [13]. Chinese culture honours parents out of gratitude (孝 xiào); worship is gratitude to the Source of everything.

中文: 真主不需要任何东西。崇拜是为了我们自己，不是为了他。就像你孝敬父母，是因为感恩。崇拜是对给了我们一切的主的感恩。(Zhēnzhǔ bù xūyào rènhé dōngxi. Chóngbài shì wèi le wǒmen zìjǐ, bú shì wèi le tā. Jiù xiàng nǐ xiàojìng fùmǔ, shì yīnwèi gǎn'ēn. Chóngbài shì duì gěi le wǒmen yíqiè de Zhǔ de gǎn'ēn.) “God needs nothing. Worship is for our own sake, not for His. Just as you honour your parents out of gratitude, worship is gratitude to the Lord who gave us everything.”বাংলা আল্লাহর কোনো কিছুর প্রয়োজন নেই। ইবাদত আমাদের নিজেদের জন্য, তাঁর জন্য নয়। যেমন তুমি কৃতজ্ঞতা থেকে বাবা-মায়ের সেবা ও সম্মান করো, তেমনি ইবাদত হলো সেই প্রভুর প্রতি কৃতজ্ঞতা, যিনি আমাদের সবকিছু দিয়েছেন।

Q13. “A loving God would not send people to hell.” 慈爱的神不会把人送进地狱

বাংলা: “দয়াময় সৃষ্টিকর্তা মানুষকে জাহান্নামে পাঠাবেন না।”

A loving God is also a just God. Would you call a judge “loving” if he let every murderer walk free? God's mercy is greater: “My mercy prevails over My wrath” [14]; He forgives all sins for anyone who sincerely repents (39:53); and those whom the message never clearly reached are not punished for it (17:15). Hell is for knowing, persistent rejection and evil, after reason, conscience and messengers were given [15]. Islam does not say good non-Muslims and evil people are the same; God judges each person by what they knew and what they did.

中文: 慈爱的主也是公正的主。如果法官让所有杀人犯都自由，你会说他慈爱吗？真主原谅所有真心悔改的人。没有听到过信息的人，不会因此被惩罚。(Cí'ài de Zhǔ yě shì gōngzhèng de Zhǔ. Rúguǒ fǎguān ràng suǒyǒu shārénfàn dōu zìyóu, nǐ huì shuō tā cí'ài ma? Zhēnzhǔ yuánliàng suǒyǒu zhēnxīn huǐgǎi de rén. Méiyǒu tīng dào guo xìnxī de rén, bú huì yīncǐ bèi chéngfá.) “The loving Lord is also the just Lord. If a judge set every murderer free, would you call him loving? God forgives everyone who sincerely repents. Those who never heard the message will not be punished for that.”বাংলা দয়াময় প্রভু ন্যায়বিচারকও। কোনো বিচারক যদি সব খুনিকে ছেড়ে দেন, তুমি কি তাঁকে দয়ালু বলবে? যে আন্তরিকভাবে তওবা করে, আল্লাহ তাকে ক্ষমা করেন। যারা কখনো এই বার্তা শোনেনি, তাদের সে কারণে শাস্তি দেওয়া হবে না।

Q14. “If God knows everything, we have no free will.” 真主知道一切，那我们没有自由

বাংলা: “আল্লাহ যদি সব জানেন, তাহলে তো আমাদের স্বাধীনতা নেই।”

Knowing is not forcing. A teacher who knows a lazy student will fail did not make him fail. God is outside time; He sees your choice but does not make it for you. The Quran says “whoever wills, let him believe, and whoever wills, let him disbelieve” (18:29) [16]. The atheist has the bigger problem: if we are only atoms following physical laws, there is no free will at all, and even his atheism was not freely chosen.

4.2 The Quran is preserved

  • The Birmingham Quran manuscript has been radiocarbon-dated to 568-645 CE, and its text matches today's Quran [1]. The Tubingen manuscript dates to 649-675 CE and the Paris-St Petersburg codex to about 650-675 CE [2].

  • Quran 15:9: “We have sent down the Reminder, and We will surely guard it.” A 1,400-year-old promise, still kept.

4.4 The Quran's challenge

  • The Quran challenges anyone to produce one chapter like it (2:23; 10:38; 17:88). The masters of Arabic, who had every reason to answer, never did [8].
  • Muhammad could not read or write (7:157; 29:48), had no teacher, and composed no poetry before age 40. Then he recited a book that fits neither Arabic poetry nor prose and shaped the Arabic language ever since.
  • The style never changes across 23 years of revelation, through war, peace, grief and victory. The Quran also rebukes the Prophet himself (80:1-10), which an author does not do to himself.

4.5 Prophecies fulfilled

  • Rome, just defeated by Persia around 614, would win again “within a few years” (30:2-4); the Byzantine victories of 622-628 fulfilled it [9].
  • The believers would enter Mecca in safety (48:27), revealed when they had just been turned away; fulfilled in 629-630.
  • The Prophet foretold the fall of the Persian and Byzantine empires to a few hundred poor followers (Sahih al-Bukhari 3120) and that Islam would reach the east and the west (Sahih Muslim 2889). Within a century it reached China and Spain [10].

4.6 The Prophet's character

  • Known as al-Amin, “the Trustworthy”, before prophethood, even by his enemies.
  • Offered wealth and power to stop preaching; refused. He died with his armour pawned to a Jewish neighbour for barley (Sahih al-Bukhari 2916). A fraud seeks gain; he gained nothing material [11].
  • He forgave the people of Mecca who had persecuted him when he returned victorious in 630.
  • The historian W. Montgomery Watt (Oxford, 1953): “To suppose Muhammad an impostor raises more problems than it solves.” Michael Hart ranked him first in The 100 (1978) as “the only man in history who was supremely successful on both the religious and secular levels” [12].

4.7 Islam makes sense

  • One God, no trinity, no idols, no priests between you and God, no original sin, no one needs to die for your sins. You pray directly to your Creator.
  • Guidance for every part of life (family, money, food, hygiene, justice) that has worked for 1,400 years across every culture, including China's.
  • The Quran repeatedly asks “Do you not think? Do you not reflect?” Faith built on reflection, not blind obedience.
  • People raised Muslim overwhelmingly remain Muslim: retention is above 90% in most countries surveyed [13]. (Do not claim Islam grows mainly by conversion; its global growth is mostly demographic.)

Islam is not the religion of the Arabs; it belongs to all humanity.”বাংলা ইসলাম থাং রাজবংশের সময় (৬৫১ খ্রিস্টাব্দে) চীনে এসেছে, তেরোশো বছরেরও বেশি আগে। শিআনের বড় মসজিদ পুরোপুরি চীনা ধাঁচে তৈরি। চেং হ্য (Zheng He) একজন মুসলিম ছিলেন। চীনে প্রায় দুই কোটি মুসলিম আছে। ইসলাম শুধু আরবদের ধর্ম নয়, গোটা মানবজাতির।

5.2 “Why don't you eat pork?” 你为什么不吃猪肉？

বাংলা: “তুমি শূকরের মাংস খাও না কেন?”

Short answer: God forbade it (2:173; 5:3), as He did for the Jews, and we obey the Creator the way you follow a doctor you trust without knowing every reason. Islam also forbids blood, carrion and alcohol, for purity and health; pork can carry parasites such as Trichinella and tapeworm [4]. The key idea is obedience to the One who made us. Chinese people already respect food rules (many Buddhists avoid meat). Be friendly: 清真 (halal) restaurants are everywhere in China, and a lamian shop is a perfect place to meet.

中文: 因为真主禁止了。就像你相信医生，不一定知道每一个原因。伊斯兰也禁止酒、血和死肉，都是为了健康和干净。(Yīnwèi Zhēnzhǔ jìnzhǐ le. Jiù xiàng nǐ xiāngxìn yīshēng, bù yídìng zhīdào měi yí ge yuányīn. Yīsīlán yě jìnzhǐ jiǔ, xuè hé sǐròu, dōu shì wèi le jiànkāng hé gānjìng.) “Because God forbade it. Just as you trust a doctor without necessarily knowing every reason. Islam also forbids alcohol, blood and carrion, all for health and cleanliness.”বাংলা কারণ আল্লাহ এটা নিষেধ করেছেন। যেমন তুমি ডাক্তারকে বিশ্বাস করো, সব কারণ না জেনেও। ইসলাম মদ, রক্ত আর মরা প্রাণীর মাংসও নিষেধ করেছে; এসবই স্বাস্থ্য আর পবিত্রতার জন্য।

5.3 “Why do Muslim women cover their hair? Is Islam against women?” 为什么穆斯林女人要戴头巾？

বাংলা: “মুসলিম নারীরা কেন হিজাব (মাথার স্কার্ফ) পরে?”

Hijab is modesty and dignity: “judge me by my mind and character, not my body”. The Quran commands men to lower their gaze first, then women (24:30-31). Islam gave women the right to own property, inherit (4:7), receive the dower as their own (4:4), consent to marriage, and keep their own family name, 1,400 years ago [5]. The Prophet said: “The best of you are those who are best to their families” and, of a mother, “stay with her, for Paradise is beneath her feet” [6]. Where women are mistreated, that is local culture breaking Islamic law. Traditional Chinese culture also valued modesty (端庄).

中文: 头巾是女人自己的选择，代表端庄和尊严，意思是“看我的品格，不要看我的身体”。伊斯兰在一千四百年前就给了女人财产权、继承权和选择丈夫的权利。(Tóujīn shì nǚrén zìjǐ de xuǎnzé, dàibiǎo duānzhuāng hé zūnyán, yìsi shì “kàn wǒ de pǐngé, bú yào kàn wǒ de shēntǐ”. Yīsīlán zài yì qiān sì bǎi nián qián jiù gěi le nǚrén cáichǎnquán, jìchéngquán hé xuǎnzé zhàngfu de quánlì.) “The headscarf is a woman's own choice. It stands for modesty and dignity, meaning “look at my character, not my body”. 1,400 years ago Islam gave women the right to property, to inheritance and to choose their husband.”বাংলা হিজাব নারীর নিজের পছন্দ; এটা শালীনতা ও মর্যাদার চিহ্ন, এর মানে: “আমার চরিত্র দেখো, আমার শরীর নয়।” ইসলাম চৌদ্দশো বছর আগেই নারীদের সম্পত্তির অধিকার, উত্তরাধিকারের অধিকার আর স্বামী বেছে নেওয়ার অধিকার দিয়েছে।

5.4 “Isn't Islam violent? What about terrorism?” 伊斯兰不是很暴力吗？

বাংলা: “ইসলাম কি খুব সহিংস নয়?”

The Quran says killing one innocent person is like killing all humanity (5:32), forbids forcing anyone into religion (2:256), and commands justice even toward people you dislike (5:8). Fighting is permitted only in defence against aggression (22:39-40; 2:190), and the first caliph, Abu Bakr, ordered armies never to harm women, children, the elderly, monks, trees or animals [7]. Terrorists kill mostly Muslims and are condemned by Muslim scholars worldwide. Nearly two billion Muslims live peacefully; judge a religion by its book and its best followers, not its worst. In China, do not discuss Xinjiang or politics; stay with the teachings.

中文: 《古兰经》说，杀一个无辜的人就像杀了全人类。宗教不能强迫。恐怖分子杀的大多数是穆斯林，伊斯兰学者都谴责他们。世界上将近二十亿穆斯林，绝大多数都是和平的。(《Gǔlánjīng》 shuō, shā yí ge wúgū de rén jiù xiàng shā le quán rénlèi. Zōngjiào bù néng qiǎngpò. Kǒngbù fènzǐ shā de dàduōshù shì Mùsīlín, Yīsīlán xuézhě dōu qiǎnzé tāmen. Shìjiè shang jiāngjìn èrshí yì Mùsīlín, jué dàduōshù dōu shì hépíng de.) “The Quran says killing one innocent person is like killing all humanity. Religion cannot be forced. Most of the people terrorists kill are Muslims, and Islamic scholars all condemn them. Of nearly two billion Muslims in the world, the vast majority are peaceful.”বাংলা কুরআন বলে, একজন নিরপরাধ মানুষকে হত্যা করা যেন গোটা মানবজাতিকে হত্যা করা। ধর্মে জোরজবরদস্তি নেই। সন্ত্রাসীরা যাদের মারে, তাদের বেশিরভাগই মুসলিম, আর ইসলামি আলেমরা সবাই তাদের নিন্দা করেন। পৃথিবীর প্রায় দুইশো কোটি মুসলিমের বিশাল অংশই শান্তিপ্রিয়।

5.5 “Why pray five times a day? Isn't that too much?” 为什么一天礼拜五次？

বাংলা: “দিনে পাঁচবার নামাজ কেন?”

Each prayer takes about five minutes. It is a reset: you stop, wash and remember why you exist. Chinese people practise tai chi every morning for the body; prayer is the same discipline for the soul. Research reviews link regular religious practice with lower depression and better coping [8]. It also keeps a person honest all day: it is hard to cheat at two o'clock when you will stand before God at three.

中文: 每次礼拜只要五分钟。就像你们每天练太极是为了身体，礼拜是为了心灵。它让人一整天都记得自己是谁、为什么活着。(Měi cì lǐbài zhǐ yào wǔ fēnzhōng. Jiù xiàng nǐmen měi tiān liàn tàijí shì wèi le shēntǐ, lǐbài shì wèi le xīnlíng. Tā ràng rén yì zhěng tiān dōu jìde zìjǐ shì shéi, wèishénme huózhe.) “Each prayer takes only five minutes. Just as you practise tai chi every day for the body, prayer is for the soul. It helps a person remember all day who they are and why they are alive.”বাংলা প্রতিবার নামাজে মাত্র পাঁচ মিনিট লাগে। তোমরা যেমন শরীরের জন্য প্রতিদিন তাইচি করো, নামাজ তেমনি আত্মার জন্য। এটা মানুষকে সারাদিন মনে রাখতে সাহায্য করে সে কে আর কেন বেঁচে আছে।

5.6 “What about my parents and ancestors? Does Islam respect family?” 伊斯兰尊重家庭吗？

বাংলা: “ইসলাম কি পরিবারকে সম্মান করে?”

Strongly. The Quran places kindness to parents right after the worship of God (17:23-24) and commands honouring them even if they are not Muslim (31:14-15). Visiting relatives and caring for the elderly are acts of worship. Islam forbids only praying to ancestors, because prayer belongs to God alone; remembering them, visiting their graves and giving charity in their name are encouraged. Chinese Muslim scholars summed this up as 忠主孝亲, “loyal to the Lord, filial to parents” [3].

中文: 《古兰经》把孝敬父母放在崇拜真主之后。父母不是穆斯林也要孝敬。伊斯兰只是不允许向祖先祈祷，因为祈祷只属于真主。纪念和尊重祖先是可以的。(《Gǔlánjīng》 bǎ xiàojìng fùmǔ fàng zài chóngbài Zhēnzhǔ zhī hòu. Fùmǔ bú shì Mùsīlín yě yào xiàojìng. Yīsīlán zhǐshì bù yǔnxǔ xiàng zǔxiān qídǎo, yīnwèi qídǎo zhǐ shǔyú Zhēnzhǔ. Jìniàn hé zūnzhòng zǔxiān shì kěyǐ de.) “The Quran places honouring parents right after worshipping God. Even if your parents are not Muslim, you must honour them. Islam only forbids praying to ancestors, because prayer belongs to God alone. Remembering and respecting ancestors is allowed.”বাংলা কুরআন আল্লাহর ইবাদতের পরেই বাবা-মায়ের সেবা ও সম্মানের কথা বলেছে। বাবা-মা মুসলিম না হলেও তাঁদের সেবা ও সম্মান করতে হবে। ইসলাম শুধু পূর্বপুরুষদের কাছে প্রার্থনা করা নিষেধ করে, কারণ প্রার্থনা কেবল আল্লাহর প্রাপ্য। পূর্বপুরুষদের স্মরণ ও সম্মান করা যায়।

5.7 “How is Islam different from Buddhism?” 伊斯兰和佛教有什么不同？

বাংলা: “ইসলাম আর বৌদ্ধধর্মের মধ্যে পার্থক্য কী?”

Buddhism, as most Chinese know it, has no Creator, teaches rebirth, and seeks release from suffering through detachment. Islam teaches one Creator, one life, one judgement, and a life lived fully in the world (marriage, work, family) in service of God. Both value compassion, discipline and honesty. Buddhism asks “how do I stop suffering?”; Islam asks “who made me and what does He want?”. Many Chinese like that Islam has no statues, no monks and no middlemen.

中文: 佛教没有创造者，讲轮回。伊斯兰讲一个创造者、一次生命、一次审判。佛教问：怎么摆脱痛苦？伊斯兰问：谁创造了我，他要我做什么？(Fójiào méiyǒu chuàngzàozhě, jiǎng lúnhuí. Yīsīlán jiǎng yí ge chuàngzàozhě, yí cì shēngmìng, yí cì shěnpàn. Fójiào wèn: zěnme bǎituō tòngkǔ? Yīsīlán wèn: shéi chuàngzào le wǒ, tā yào wǒ zuò shénme?) “Buddhism has no Creator and teaches rebirth. Islam teaches one Creator, one life, one judgement. Buddhism asks: how do I escape suffering? Islam asks: who created me, and what does He want me to do?”বাংলা বৌদ্ধধর্মে কোনো স্রষ্টা নেই, এটি পুনর্জন্মের কথা বলে। ইসলাম বলে একজন স্রষ্টা, একটি জীবন, একবার বিচার। বৌদ্ধধর্ম জিজ্ঞেস করে: দুঃখ থেকে কীভাবে মুক্তি পাব? ইসলাম জিজ্ঞেস করে: কে আমাকে সৃষ্টি করেছেন, আর তিনি আমার কাছে কী চান?

5.8 “Can I believe in God but not follow any religion?” 我可以信神但不信教吗？

বাংলা: “আমি কি কোনো ধর্ম না মেনে শুধু সৃষ্টিকর্তায় বিশ্বাস করতে পারি?”

That is a good first step; the fitrah is awake. Then ask: if the Creator made you on purpose, would He leave you with no instructions? A company that hires you gives you a job description. Follow the evidence to the message He sent. Islam does not ask for blind faith: read the Quran in Ma Jian's Chinese translation, legally sold at mosques in China, and judge for yourself [9].

中文: 这是很好的第一步。但是，如果创造者有目的地创造了你，他会不给你任何指示吗？你可以自己读马坚翻译的《古兰经》，自己判断。(Zhè shì hěn hǎo de dì-yī bù. Dànshì, rúguǒ chuàngzàozhě yǒu mùdì de chuàngzào le nǐ, tā huì bù gěi nǐ rènhé zhǐshì ma? Nǐ kěyǐ zìjǐ dú Mǎ Jiān fānyì de 《Gǔlánjīng》, zìjǐ pànduàn.) “This is a very good first step. But if the Creator created you with a purpose, would He give you no instructions at all? You can read Ma Jian's translation of the Quran and judge for yourself.”বাংলা এটা খুব ভালো প্রথম ধাপ। কিন্তু স্রষ্টা যদি উদ্দেশ্য নিয়ে তোমাকে সৃষ্টি করে থাকেন, তিনি কি তোমাকে কোনো নির্দেশনাই দেবেন না? তুমি নিজে মা চিয়েনের (Ma Jian) অনুবাদ করা কুরআন পড়ে নিজেই বিচার করতে পারো।

6.3 Sayings not to attribute to the Prophet

These circulate widely in dawah talks but are weak or fabricated. Quoting them to an educated listener damages your credibility [3].

  • “Seek knowledge even in China.” Very weak; al-Albani graded it baseless. You can say: “A famous saying, though not authentic, links seeking knowledge with China”, or simply leave it out.
  • “The ink of the scholar is holier than the blood of the martyr.” Fabricated.
  • “Paradise lies at the feet of mothers” in that exact short wording is weak; use the Nasa'i version above.

中文 Pinyin English বাংলা

希望你自己思考一下。 Xīwàng nǐ zìjǐ sīkǎo yíxià. I hope you will think about it yourself. আশা করি তুমি নিজে একটু ভেবে দেখবে।
如果你有兴趣，我们可以一起去清真寺看看。 Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu xìngqù, wǒmen kěyǐ yìqǐ qù qīngzhēnsì kànkan. If you are interested, we can visit a mosque together. তোমার আগ্রহ থাকলে আমরা একসঙ্গে মসজিদ দেখতে যেতে পারি।
我们一起去吃清真菜吧。 Wǒmen yìqǐ qù chī qīngzhēn cài ba. Let's go and eat halal food together. চলো, একসঙ্গে হালাল খাবার খেতে যাই।
不管你信不信，我们都是朋友。 Bùguǎn nǐ xìn bú xìn, wǒmen dōu shì péngyou. Whether you believe or not, we are friends. তুমি বিশ্বাস করো বা না করো, আমরা বন্ধু।

7.4 The one-minute message

Memorise this. Part V, Lesson 12, explains it word by word.

我相信宇宙有一个创造者，因为宇宙有开始，而且非常精密。创造者只有一个，他没有形象，没有伙伴。他创造我们是有目的的：认识他、感谢他、做好人。他派了很多先知，最后一个是穆罕默德，带来了《古兰经》。《古兰经》一千四百年没有改变过。这就是伊斯兰。很简单，很理性。如果你有兴趣，我们可以慢慢聊。

Wǒ xiāngxìn yǔzhòu yǒu yí ge chuàngzàozhě, yīnwèi yǔzhòu yǒu kāishǐ, érqiě fēicháng jīngmì. Chuàngzàozhě zhǐ yǒu yí ge, tā méiyǒu xíngxiàng, méiyǒu huǒbàn. Tā chuàngzào wǒmen shì yǒu mùdì de: rènshi tā, gǎnxiè tā, zuò hǎorén. Tā pài le hěn duō xiānzhī, zuìhòu yí ge shì Mùhǎnmòdé, dàilái le 《Gǔlánjīng》. 《Gǔlánjīng》 yì qiān sì bǎi nián méiyǒu gǎibiàn guo. Zhè jiù shì Yīsīlán. Hěn jiǎndān, hěn lǐxìng. Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu xìngqù, wǒmen kěyǐ mànmān liáo.

“I believe the universe has a Creator, because it had a beginning and it is extremely precise. There is only one Creator; He has no image and no partner. He created us for a purpose: to know Him, thank Him and be good people. He sent many prophets; the last was Muhammad, who brought the Quran. The Quran has not changed in 1,400 years. That is Islam: very simple, very rational. If you are interested, we can talk more over time.”বাংলা আমি বিশ্বাস করি মহাবিশ্বের একজন স্রষ্টা আছেন, কারণ মহাবিশ্বের শুরু আছে এবং এটা অত্যন্ত নিখুঁত। স্রষ্টা একজনই; তাঁর কোনো আকৃতি নেই, কোনো শরিক নেই। তিনি আমাদের উদ্দেশ্য নিয়ে সৃষ্টি করেছেন: তাঁকে চেনা, তাঁর শুকরিয়া আদায় করা আর ভালো মানুষ হওয়া। তিনি অনেক নবী পাঠিয়েছেন; শেষ নবী মুহাম্মাদ (সা.), যিনি কুরআন নিয়ে এসেছেন। চৌদ্দশো বছরে কুরআন একটুও বদলায়নি। এটাই ইসলাম: খুব সহজ, খুব যুক্তিসংগত। তোমার আগ্রহ থাকলে আমরা ধীরে ধীরে কথা বলতে পারি।

সারকথা: চীনা বন্ধুর সঙ্গে কথা শুরু করা, চালিয়ে যাওয়া আর আন্তরিকভাবে শেষ করার সহজ বাক্য এবং এক মিনিটের বার্তা এখানে আছে। সঠিক টোনে জোরে জোরে অনুশীলন করো, তাহলে এক মিনিটে চীনা ভাষায় ইসলামের মূল কথা বলতে পারবে।

Chapter 8: When someone wants to become Muslim, and your own character

🔊 Listen to this section · শুনুন

A dawah book is incomplete if it stops at the argument. This chapter covers the moment a friend says “I want to know more” or “I want to become Muslim”, how to handle it inside Chinese law, what a new Muslim needs in the first months, and the character that makes your words believable.

8.1 Do not rush

“There is no compulsion in religion” (2:256). A decision made under pressure does not last. Make sure your friend understands three things before anything else: there is one God with no partner; Muhammad is His final messenger; and Islam is a way of life, not only a belief. If they still have serious doubts, keep talking; there is no deadline.

8.2 Explaining the shahada

The testimony of faith (清真言 qīngzhēnyán) is the door into Islam.

  • Arabic: Ash-hadu an lā ilāha illa-llāh, wa ash-hadu anna Muḥammadan rasūlu-llāh.
  • 中文: 我作证：万物非主，唯有真主；我又作证：穆罕默德是真主的使者。(Wǒ zuòzhèng: wànwù fēi zhǔ, wéi yǒu Zhēnzhǔ; wǒ yòu zuòzhèng: Mùhǎnmòdé shì Zhēnzhǔ de shǐzhě.)বাংলা আমি সাক্ষ্য দিচ্ছি যে আল্লাহ ছাড়া কোনো ইলাহ নেই, এবং আমি আরও সাক্ষ্য দিচ্ছি যে মুহাম্মাদ (সা.) আল্লাহর রাসূল।
  • English: I bear witness that there is no god but God, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the messenger of God.

Explain the six articles of faith (六信: 信真主、信天仙、信经典、信使者、信后世、信前定; belief in God, angels, scriptures, messengers, the Last Day and divine decree) and the five pillars (五功: 念、礼、斋、课、朝) as they appear in the hadith of Jibril [1].বাংলা 六信 = ঈমানের ছয়টি বিষয়: আল্লাহ, ফেরেশতা, আসমানি কিতাব, রাসূল, আখিরাত ও তাকদিরে বিশ্বাস। 五功 = ইসলামের পাঁচ স্তম্ভ: 念 কালিমা, 礼 নামাজ, 斋 রোজা, 课 জাকাত, 朝 হজ।

8.3 The lawful path in China

As a foreigner you must not “develop followers among Chinese citizens” or run religious classes for them (Chapter 1, section 1.2). The right path is also the best path:

  1. Introduce your friend to the ahong (阿訇, imam) at a registered mosque in your city. Go with them the first time if they want.
  2. Let the ahong guide the shahada and the first lessons. Chinese imams have welcomed Han Chinese into Islam for centuries and know the language, the family questions and the local rules far better than you.
  3. Connect them to the local Muslim community, especially Hui families, who can support them in Chinese.
  4. Remain their friend. Friendship is not regulated; keep eating together, answering questions, and caring for them.

中文: 如果你想更多了解，我可以带你去清真寺见阿訇，他会用中文给你讲得比我更清楚。(Rúguǒ nǐ xiǎng gèng duō liǎojiě, wǒ kěyǐ dài nǐ qù qīngzhēnsì jiàn āhōng, tā huì yòng Zhōngwén gěi nǐ jiǎng de bǐ wǒ gèng qīngchu.) “If you want to know more, I can take you to the mosque to meet the ahong; he will explain it in Chinese more clearly than I can.”বাংলা তুমি যদি আরও জানতে চাও, আমি তোমাকে মসজিদে নিয়ে গিয়ে আহোংয়ের (ইমাম) সঙ্গে দেখা করিয়ে দিতে পারি; তিনি চীনা ভাষায় আমার চেয়ে আরও পরিষ্কার করে বুঝিয়ে বলবেন।

8.4 The first months of a new Muslim

The Prophet said: “Make things easy and do not make them difficult; give good news and do not drive people away” [2].

  • One step at a time. First the shahada and the basic belief; then learning to pray, starting with al-Fatiha; then halal food. Do not hand over a list of fifty rules on day one.
  • Family first. Honour non-Muslim parents and stay close to them (31:14-15). Many Han converts face worried families; patience and kindness are the answer, not argument.
  • Keep the Chinese name. A new Muslim does not need to change their name unless its meaning is bad. They may add a Muslim name if they wish.
  • Keep life stable. Keep their job, their studies and their friends. Islam is lived inside ordinary Chinese life, as Hui Muslims have done for centuries.
  • Community. Encourage Friday prayer at the mosque, iftar in Ramadan, and friendship with practising Chinese Muslims of their own age.
  • Learning in Chinese. Ma Jian's Quran, the Chinese edition of A Brief Illustrated Guide to Understanding Islam, and the teaching of the local ahong (Part IV, section 6).

8.5 When someone says no

Stay friends. “For you your religion, and for me mine” (109:6). Many people need years; some accept Islam long after the first conversation, often because a Muslim friend never stopped being kind. Never insult, never pressure, never stop caring.

中文: 不管你信不信，我们都是朋友。(Bùguǎn nǐ xìn bú xìn, wǒmen dōu shì péngyou.) “Whether you believe or not, we are friends.”বাংলা তুমি বিশ্বাস করো বা না করো, আমরা বন্ধু।

8.6 Your own character is the strongest evidence

“Who is better in speech than one who invites to God, does good, and says ‘I am a Muslim’? Repel evil with what is better, and your enemy will become like a close friend” (41:33-34). The Prophet said he was sent “only to perfect good character” [3].

  • Be honest in small things: money, time, promises, work. Chinese colleagues notice.
  • Control anger (Sahih al-Bukhari 6114) and smile (Jami' al-Tirmidhi 1956).
  • Keep learning. Study with a qualified scholar; check every claim before you repeat it (Part II).
  • Make du'a for the people you talk to. Guidance is from God alone (28:56). A simple prayer: Allahumma-hdihim, “O God, guide them.”
  • Be patient. The Prophet Nuh called his people for 950 years (29:14). Your success is in your effort and sincerity, not in numbers.
