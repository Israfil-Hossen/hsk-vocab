IntroductionKnowing the Creator 认识真主

Preface

বইটির উদ্দেশ্য · What this book is for

এই বইয়ের দুটি লক্ষ্য:

  1. চাইনিজ শেখা, একদম শূন্য থেকে HSK 4: উচ্চারণ, টোন ও অক্ষর লেখা (Part 0), শব্দভান্ডার, ব্যাকরণ, অডিও QR কোড দিয়ে শোনা, Practice Ladder দিয়ে বলা, আর Appendix D-তে HSK পরীক্ষার গাইড।
  2. পূর্ণাঙ্গ, বৈধ ও বন্ধুত্বভিত্তিক দাওয়াত: কেন আল্লাহয় বিশ্বাস করব, প্রমাণ, আপত্তির উত্তর, চীনের প্রেক্ষাপট, আর তৈরি কথোপকথন।

প্রতিটা চাইনিজ বাক্যের সাথে পিনইন, ইংরেজি ও বাংলা অর্থ আছে, আর প্রতিটা অংশ শেষ হয় একটা বাংলা সারকথা দিয়ে।

This book has two goals:

  1. Learn Chinese from zero to HSK 4: pronunciation, tones and characters (Part 0), vocabulary, grammar, listening with the audio QR codes, speaking with the Practice Ladder, and the HSK exam guide in Appendix D.
  2. Carry out complete, lawful, friendship-based dawah: why believe, the evidence, answers to objections, the China context, and ready-made conversations.

Every Chinese sentence has pinyin, English and Bengali, and every section ends with a Bengali summary.

This book was written for a Muslim living in China who wants to answer two kinds of questions: the questions of people who do not believe in God, and the questions Chinese people ask about Islam. It was also written for someone still learning Chinese, so the language is taught alongside the message.

Three convictions shaped it. First, truth does not need exaggeration: every piece of evidence in this book is graded, and claims that careful Muslim scholars themselves reject have been left out or marked. Second, character comes before argument: most people are moved by a trustworthy friend long before they are moved by a proof. Third, China is not foreign soil for Islam: Muslims have lived here since 651, and Chinese Muslim scholars explained Islam in the language of Confucius more than three centuries ago. This book follows their path.

It is a working edition. Before you print or teach from it, ask a qualified scholar to review the religious content and a native Chinese speaker to review the Chinese (see the pre-publication checklist in Appendix B).

সারকথা: বইটি চীনে থাকা একজন মুসলিমের জন্য, যে নাস্তিক ও চীনা বন্ধুদের প্রশ্নের উত্তর দিতে চায় আর একই সাথে চাইনিজ শিখছে। তিনটি নীতি: সত্যে অতিরঞ্জন নেই, যুক্তির আগে চরিত্র, আর চীন ইসলামের জন্য অপরিচিত মাটি নয়।

From zero to HSK 4: a one-year path

What this book can do, and what it cannot do alone. It can teach you the sounds and tones, how characters work, the 1,089 HSK 1–4 words, the grammar of each level and the HSK exam format. It can give you dawah language at every level, and audio for every Chinese word in it. It cannot hear you. No book can correct your tones or your speaking, so you also need a teacher or a language partner. For full HSK exam practice, add the official sample papers and a mock test before each exam. If you can, study alongside a Chinese class or the standard HSK textbooks. This book then becomes the place where your Chinese and your dawah meet.

সৎ কথা: এই বই দিয়ে একদম শূন্য থেকে শুরু করে HSK 4 পর্যন্ত যাওয়া যায়, আর প্রতিটা লেভেলে দাওয়াহর ভাষা শেখা যায়। কিন্তু বই তোমার উচ্চারণ শুনতে পারে না। তাই সপ্তাহে অন্তত একবার একজন শিক্ষক বা ভাষা-সঙ্গীর (语伴) সাথে কথা বলো, আর প্রতিটা পরীক্ষার আগে একটা mock test দাও।

Every week. Study six days, 30–45 minutes a day: new words in the app, one section of the book, and five minutes of listening on the audio pages. On Friday, go to Jumu'ah and practise your phrases with Chinese Muslims. On Saturday, meet your language partner. On Sunday, review: do the exercises, tick the can-do list and take a short mock test.

Find a language partner (语伴 yǔbàn). Most universities have Chinese students who want to practise English. A partner of the same gender is easiest for a Muslim student.

我们可以做语伴吗？我教你英语，你教我中文。(Wǒmen kěyǐ zuò yǔbàn ma? Wǒ jiāo nǐ Yīngyǔ, nǐ jiāo wǒ Zhōngwén.) "Can we be language partners? I'll teach you English and you teach me Chinese."বাংলা আমরা কি ভাষা-সঙ্গী হতে পারি? আমি তোমাকে ইংরেজি শেখাব, তুমি আমাকে চাইনিজ শেখাবে।

সারকথা: এক বছরের পথ: প্রথম মাসে শূন্য থেকে HSK 1, তিন মাসে HSK 2, ছয় মাসে HSK 3, বারো মাসে HSK 4। প্রতিটা ধাপে বই, অ্যাপ আর দাওয়াহর দক্ষতা একসাথে চলে। সপ্তাহে একবার ভাষা-সঙ্গীর সাথে কথা বলা বাধ্যতামূলক মনে করো।

How to use this book: a 12-week plan

কোথা থেকে শুরু করবে? চাইনিজ একদম না জানলে আগে Part 0 (চার সপ্তাহ), তারপর নিচের এক বছরের পথ। তোমার চাইনিজ যদি HSK 1–3 হয়, প্রথমে Part VI-এর ১২ সপ্তাহের roadmap (VI.12) অনুসরণ করো: প্রতিদিন ২০ মিনিট। Part VI-এর Stage 3 শেষ হলে নিচের ১২ সপ্তাহের পরিকল্পনায় যাও, যেখানে Part V-এর পাঠ আর Part I-এর যুক্তি একসাথে চলে। Where to start: with no Chinese at all, begin with Part 0 (four weeks) and follow the one-year path below; at HSK 1–3, follow the Part VI roadmap first (VI.12, 20 minutes a day). When you finish Stage 3, move to the plan below.

Sources, evidence grades and abbreviations

Citation style. Numbers in square brackets, like [3], point to the numbered notes at the end of each chapter of Part I. Parts II, III and IV give their sources beside each item and in a Sources section at the end. Appendix B collects every source in one bibliography.

Quran references are written surah:verse, so 52:35 means Surah 52, verse 35. English meanings follow Sahih International in simplified form; Chinese follows Ma Jian's translation. Lines marked * are close renderings of the meaning that must be checked against a printed Ma Jian Quran.

Hadith references give the collection and the number used on sunnah.com, with the grade where it matters:

Grade Meaning বাংলা Use
Sahih 健全 Authentic সহিহ (বিশুদ্ধ) Quote freely
Hasan 良好 Good হাসান (ভালো, গ্রহণযোগ্য) Quote freely
Da'if 弱 Weak জয়িফ (দুর্বল) Do not attribute to the Prophet
Mawdu' 伪造 Fabricated মাওজু (জাল) Never quote

Evidence grades (Part II): A = plain Arabic, use freely; B = reasonable but interpretive, present as a possible reading; C = depends on one translation or is disputed, avoid with sceptics. Verification labels: S = confirmed through a quoted extract of the source page; K = standard content of the named book, not re-confirmed; Unverified = could not be traced; Drop = rejected by careful scholars or shown false.

How the research was done. The sources were collected on 27 October 2026 from the free dawah literature, academic publications and survey organisations. The research tools could search the web but could not open the pages in full, so each source was confirmed through the search engine's quoted extract. Open each link and check the wording before you cite it publicly.

Disclaimer, legal note and scholarly review

  • Not a fatwa. This book summarises what established Muslim scholars and institutions have written; it is not a ruling. On any question of belief or practice, follow a qualified scholar.

  • Not legal advice. Part I, Chapter 1 and Part IV, section 4 summarise the published rules on foreigners' religious activity in China as of October 2026. Rules and their enforcement change; check them yourself. This book is for your own study. Do not copy, print or distribute it to Chinese citizens, and do not post its content on Chinese platforms.

  • Chinese language. The Chinese was prepared for a learner and has not yet been checked by a native speaker. Have a Chinese Muslim or teacher correct it before you rely on it in public.

  • Quran and hadith. Check every Chinese verse against Ma Jian's printed translation and every hadith number at sunnah.com before quoting.

  • Humility. If anything in this book is correct, it is from God; any error is from its compilers. Corrections are welcome and will be added to the next edition.
