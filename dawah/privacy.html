Privacy policy

Dawah Chinese · 认识真主 · Knowing the Creator

Effective date: 30 October 2026. Developer: Mohammad Israfil Hossain.

We do not collect any personal data. The Dawah Chinese app and website have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device. Your study progress (words learned, review schedule, sections read, daily goal, streak, reminder time and voice settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data deletes it.

Speech. Chinese audio is spoken by your device's own text-to-speech engine or played from audio files inside the app. The text-to-speech engine is provided by your device maker or Google under their own terms.

Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show one notification a day. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time.

Links. Some buttons open other websites or apps (for example, sharing to WhatsApp, or downloading the PDF from our website). Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children. The app is intended for university students and adults.

Changes and contact. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
